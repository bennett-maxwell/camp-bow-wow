Franchise Opportunity Profile

Camp Bow Wow

Camp Bow Wow is a pet care franchise (dog day care, boarding, grooming, training) headquartered in Westminster, Colorado. Every fact on this page is sourced from the Camp Bow Wow Franchising, Inc. 2025 Franchise Disclosure Document (issued May 1, 2025; amended November 20, 2025), with Item and page citations.

Key Facts

Initial Franchise Fee

$50,000

Uniform, non-refundable; includes tuition for you plus one designee at initial training. Additional camp $35,000; qualified veterans and first responders $25,000. (FDD Item 5, p.21)

Estimated Initial Investment

$943,606 to $1,199,536

$55,000-$55,500 of that is payable to the franchisor or affiliates. Multi-unit (3 units): $1,104,306-$1,325,036. (FDD cover, p.1; Item 7, pp.31-32)

Royalty

3.5% yr 1, then 7%

3.5% of Net Revenue for the first 12 months (in good standing); thereafter the greater of 7% of Net Revenue or the Minimum Monthly Royalty ($0-$2,500/month by tenure). Payable twice monthly via ACH. (FDD Item 6, p.22)

Advertising Fund

1% to 3%

1% of Net Revenue up to a maximum of 3%, payable twice monthly. Local advertising $2,500/month if the franchisor collects it. (FDD Item 6, p.22)

Territory

Non-exclusive

Authorized Territory defined by demographics; franchisor reserves non-traditional locations, internet/digital, and acquisition rights — inside or outside your territory. Territory may be reduced at renewal. (FDD Item 12, pp.51-54)

Term

10 years + 2 x 10-year renewals

Renewal fee: 50% of the then-current initial franchise fee. Franchisee may terminate only for good cause. Mediation then arbitration in Dallas, Texas; Texas law. (FDD Item 17, pp.61-67)

Facility

~6,000 sq ft

Prototype of approximately 6,000 square feet, usually in a light industrial or commercial area; real estate improvements $516,735-$663,917. (FDD Item 11, p.42; Item 7, p.31)

Training

80 hours + 32 opening-week

Initial Camp Services Training: 48 classroom + 32 on-the-job hours; 32 hours of opening-week support; 200+ hours of online modules. Manager or Majority Owner must be full-time. (FDD Item 11, pp.49-51)

Time to Open

~18 months

Camps typically open around 18 months after signing; camp site must be acquired within 365 days of signing. (FDD Item 11, p.42)

Owner Model

Manager-run permitted

Not obligated to operate personally, but always responsible for operations; Manager or Majority Owner must devote full time and best efforts. (FDD Item 15, p.60)

HQ

Westminster, Colorado

Camp Bow Wow Franchising, Inc., 7577 West 103rd Avenue, Unit 209, Westminster, CO 80021. 877-700-BARK. Franchising since August 2014. (FDD Item 1, p.10)

System Size

224 outlets

223 franchised (222 US + 1 international) + 1 company-owned as of 12/31/2024; net +10 franchised in 2024. (FDD Item 20, pp.72-73)

Risk Profile & Disclosure

43 / 100 risk score

Assessment: Moderate. A long-tenured pet care system (predecessor operating since 2003) with audited parent financials and a parent performance guarantee, tempered by high capital intensity for a services model (nearly $1M minimum investment), a non-exclusive territory with broad franchisor reserved rights including a termination trigger tied to the minimum monthly royalty, a minimum-monthly-royalty escalator that can exceed the 7% percentage, a self-reported-only Item 19, and three resolved legal actions plus one pending Item-4 bankruptcy disclosure connected to a current officer.

Risk inputs (documented):

  • Capital intensity: investment floor $943,606, ceiling $1,199,536 — pushes score up
  • Item 4 bankruptcy disclosure: TGI Friday's Inc. filed Ch. 11 Nov 2, 2024 (N.D. Tex. 24-80069), case pending; current General Counsel served as its SVP/GC until Sept 2024 — pushes score up
  • Litigation history: 3 disclosed actions, all resolved — $12,000 settlement (2018), $129,093.80 arbitration award for the franchisor (2016), $8M shareholder-derivative settlement involving parent directors (2024) — pushes score up
  • Territory: non-exclusive; franchisor reserves non-traditional locations, internet/digital, wholesale/catalog, and M&A rights; territory may shrink at renewal; termination trigger if 4-of-12 months' revenue fails to clear the minimum royalty — pushes score up
  • Minimum monthly royalty: $0/$1,500/$1,750/$2,000/$2,500 by tenure, applies from month 7 whenever 7% of revenue falls short — pushes score up
  • Item 19 data limits: 148 of 222 locations (66.7%), self-reported, unaudited, not independently verified — pushes score up
  • Slow growth: net +8 outlets in 2024 (+3.7%); company-owned shrank 7 to 1 — pushes score up
  • Audited parent financials (Propelled Brands Franchising, LLC, FY 2022–2024) + parent performance guarantee — pushes score down
  • Brand longevity: predecessor since 2003, franchising since August 2014 — pushes score down
  • Item 19 coverage: 148 reporting locations — pushes score down
